Who is Isaac Correa?

Isaac Correa, registered as Isaac Correa Villa in the Spanish commercial register, is a Spanish entrepreneur based in Madrid. He is the founder of Hellomatik, an AI agent company, and co-founder of Kodalogic, a Looker Studio dashboard company he founded with his brother Daniel Correa. His ORCID is 0009-0003-0657-4419.

What is Hellomatik?

Hellomatik is a Madrid company that puts a client’s operating model in writing, its data, its rules, its actions and who may approve what, and then lets AI agents answer, watch and execute on top of that, inside limits the client approves in advance. Its agents answer by voice and by chat on behalf of a company. The method is published as a technical report with a DOI, not as a whitepaper.

Who founded Hellomatik, and when?

Hellomatik SL was founded in Madrid on 28 July 2025. Isaac Correa founded it at twenty-two, and his appointment as joint administrator was published that day in the Spanish commercial register, the BORME, whose source is the Agencia Estatal BOE.

Where is Hellomatik based?

In Madrid, Spain. Since 2026 the company also works with clients in the United States.

What did Isaac Correa do before Hellomatik?

Between December 2022 and November 2024 he built more than two hundred Looker Studio dashboards for clients, one commission at a time, and collected 107 client reviews, every one of them five stars. In September 2024 he and his brother Daniel founded Kodalogic to sell those dashboards finished: ready-made templates for GA4, Google Search Console and Google Ads, with the interpretation written next to each chart. He also writes the Kodalogic blog, 58 signed posts on Looker Studio reporting. The long version is here.

Are Isaac Correa and Daniel Correa related?

Yes. They are brothers, and they founded Kodalogic together in Madrid in September 2024.

What has Isaac Correa published?

“How we build a company’s ontology” (September 2026), a 26-page technical report with the DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22647735, deposited on Zenodo and Figshare and indexed under his ORCID. There is a slides version on Speaker Deck, essays on Medium, and the Kodalogic blog.

What does Hellomatik run today?

The report describes four systems in production with their limits, among them a logistics system with 24 rules over a live warehouse and a B2B sales system that recalculates 29,000 customers.
