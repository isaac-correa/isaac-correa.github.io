A hundred and fifty words

Isaac Correa is a Spanish entrepreneur based in Madrid and the co-founder of Hellomatik, founded on 28 July 2025, and of Kodalogic before it.

He started by selling: his first sale was seven dollars, on the last day of 2022, and between then and November 2024 he delivered three hundred Looker Studio dashboards for clients, one commission at a time, collecting 103 verified client reviews from the United States, Europe, South America and Korea.

His present work is the part of business automation that does not demo well: taking the logic a company already runs on and writing it down in a form a machine can evaluate, so that agents act inside limits approved in advance. He publishes the method rather than describing it — it is deposited as a technical report with a DOI, in two independent repositories, indexed under his ORCID.
