Hellomatik and Kodalogic

I have founded two companies in Madrid. This page says plainly what each one does, who it is for, and where the facts about it can be checked. The story of how they came about is on another page; this one is the description.

Hellomatik

Hellomatik puts a company’s operating model in writing: its data, its rules, its actions, and who may approve what. On top of that model, AI agents answer, watch and execute, inside limits the company approves in advance. The agents answer by voice and by chat on behalf of the business.

What that changes for a client is the order of the work. Most tools ask a company to adapt to them. This one starts by writing down how the company already decides, so that an agent applies those rules instead of guessing at them. The company keeps its own way of working, and the part that is automated is the part it has agreed to in advance.

The method is not described, it is published. How we build a company’s ontology is a 26-page technical report with the DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22647735, deposited in two independent repositories and indexed under my ORCID. It also states the scope: four systems in production, among them a logistics one with 24 rules over a live warehouse, and a B2B sales one that recalculates 29,000 customers.

I founded it on 28 July 2025, at twenty-two. My role is the one I gave myself: I think, and I run the product.

Kodalogic

Kodalogic sells ready-made reporting templates for Looker Studio, built on GA4, Google Search Console and Google Ads, with the interpretation written next to each chart. The point is not the chart: it is that the person opening the report is told what it means without having to ask anyone.

The catalogue is deliberately small. Three templates, distilled from four hundred jobs for real clients, because those four hundred jobs kept asking the same questions of the same data and starting from the same blank canvas. Building the same report two hundred times is a poor way to spend two hundred people’s money.

My brother Daniel and I founded it in Madrid in September 2024. He builds the platform; I sell and run the business. Everything Kodalogic has sold came from appearing in search results, and that took articles and patience: I wrote 58 of them, all signed.
