Using them

You can use any of these with attribution to Isaac Correa. No permission email needed and no form to fill in. If you need a different crop or a larger file, everything is served straight from this domain, so a right-click will get you the original.

What I would ask in return is only that the caption says what the picture is. A photograph of somebody in a restaurant is not a photograph of somebody at a conference, and the difference matters more than it looks when a search engine is deciding what a person does.
