Who is in them

Isaac Correa, registered in Spain as Isaac Correa Villa. Spanish, born on 17 August 2002, living and working in Madrid. Co-founder of Hellomatik, an AI agent company incorporated in Madrid on 28 July 2025, whose appointment as joint administrator was published in the BORME on 4 August 2025, sheet M-861159. Before it, co-founder of Kodalogic, founded in September 2024, which sells finished Looker Studio dashboards. ORCID 0009-0003-0657-4419.

Hellomatik puts a company’s own decisions in writing first, and then lets AI agents work on top of that record: answering, watching and carrying things out, but only within limits the company set before any of it ran.

Who took them

I did. All three are self-portraits on a timer: camera propped up, timer set, walk into the frame, check, do it again. That is why there are three and not thirty, and it is also why there is nobody to ask about rights — the subject and the photographer are the same person, so attribution to Isaac Correa covers both.

Using them

Use any of these with attribution to Isaac Correa. There is no permission email and no form to fill in. Every file is served from this domain in both WebP and JPEG, at the pixel sizes given beside each one, so a right-click gets the original and the address will not change later.

What I would ask in return is that the caption says what the picture actually is. A photograph of somebody in a restaurant is not a photograph of somebody at a conference, and captioning one as the other tells a reader something untrue about what he does.

If you need a crop, a larger file or a photograph that does not exist here yet, ask through Hellomatik.
