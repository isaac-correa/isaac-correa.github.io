Who is Isaac Correa?

Isaac Correa, registered as Isaac Correa Villa in the Spanish commercial register, is a Spanish entrepreneur based in Madrid. He is the founder of Hellomatik, an AI agent company, and co-founder of Kodalogic, a Looker Studio dashboard company he founded in September 2024. His ORCID is 0009-0003-0657-4419.

What is Hellomatik?

Hellomatik is a Madrid company that puts a client’s operating model in writing, its data, its rules, its actions and who may approve what, and then lets AI agents answer, watch and execute on top of that, inside limits the client approves in advance. Its agents answer by voice and by chat on behalf of a company. The method is published as a technical report with a DOI, not as a whitepaper.

Who founded Hellomatik, and when?

Hellomatik SL was founded in Madrid on 28 July 2025. Isaac Correa founded it at twenty-two, and his appointment as joint administrator was published that day in the Spanish commercial register, the BORME, whose source is the Agencia Estatal BOE.

Where is Hellomatik based?

In Madrid, Spain. Since 2026 the company also works with clients in the United States.

What did Isaac Correa do before Hellomatik?

Between December 2022 and November 2024 he built three hundred Looker Studio dashboards for clients, one commission at a time, and collected 103 verified client reviews. In September 2024 he founded Kodalogic to sell those dashboards finished: ready-made templates for GA4, Google Search Console and Google Ads, with the interpretation written next to each chart. He also writes the Kodalogic blog, 58 signed posts on Looker Studio reporting. The long version is here.

What has Isaac Correa published?

“How we build a company’s ontology” (September 2026), a 26-page technical report with the DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22647735, deposited on Zenodo and Figshare and indexed under his ORCID. There are essays on Medium, a blog, and the Kodalogic blog.

What does Hellomatik run today?

The report describes four systems in production with their limits, among them a logistics system with 24 rules over a live warehouse and a B2B sales system that recalculates 29,000 customers.

How can any of this be checked?

The trail is public: the commercial register entry, the ORCID record, the DOI, the Crunchbase profile and the client reviews.

How do you reach Isaac Correa?

Through hellomatik.com, or through his page at kodalogic.com/about-us/isaac-correa.

How did Isaac Correa start?

With a seven-dollar sale on 31 December 2022. Between then and November 2024 he delivered three hundred Looker Studio dashboards for clients, one commission at a time, and collected 103 verified client reviews. He sold while he studied.

Where are Isaac Correa's clients?

In the United States, Europe, South America and Korea, small businesses and companies of considerable size. At the busiest, five meetings a day.

What is Isaac Correa's role at Hellomatik?

The one he gave himself: he thinks, and he directs the product. The order he works in is data, then media, then product, and the design of the current system took two months, screen by screen.

Why does Isaac Correa publish with a DOI instead of a whitepaper?

Because a document with a version number can be cited, checked and disagreed with, and a brochure behind an email form cannot. The method is deposited at 10.5281/zenodo.22647735, in two independent repositories, indexed under his ORCID.

What does Isaac Correa say the goal looks like?

A system that understands a whole company and can act inside it, said with humility, because the cautionary version of that idea is HAL 9000. That is why the work is mostly about limits: every action carries whether it can be undone and whose approval it needs.
